MONEY BASICS, NO LECTURE

Learn it. Use it. Repeat.

Short lessons for real-life money moments — from a surprise payout to a quiet week.

📚
🧭

Needs vs. wants

Make room for essentials and still enjoy the money you worked for.

A need is important to cover, like food, travel, or a bill. A want is nice to have. List the costs you must cover before your next income arrives, then decide what is comfortable to spend.
🌦️

Plan for uneven income

A good week and a quiet week can both be part of gig work.

Plan around upcoming essentials, not just today's payout. When a payout is bigger than usual, consider setting some aside for a quieter period. Start with an amount you can realistically keep untouched.
🛟

Build a buffer

A small reserve can give you more choices when income slows down.

A buffer is money you keep for future essentials or unexpected costs. Build it little by little, and keep it separate from everyday spending if that helps. Your target depends on your expenses and situation.
🧾

Keep payout records

Simple records help you understand what you earn and what work costs.

Note the date, amount, and source each time money comes in. Keep receipts for work-related costs and check your records regularly. For specific tax questions, ask a trusted adult or qualified tax professional.
📉

Client payment signals

Spot warning signs before agreeing to a project.

Look for a clear scope, written payment dates, a reliable way to contact the client, and a record of prior payments where available. Avoid treating any score as a guarantee; a late payment can happen for many reasons.
🌱

SIP basics

Learn the idea before making any investment decisions.

A Systematic Investment Plan invests a set amount regularly into a mutual fund. Market values can rise or fall and returns are not guaranteed. Understand risk, fees, and time horizon, and speak with a trusted adult or qualified adviser before investing.
