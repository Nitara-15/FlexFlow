Money basics, no lecture

Learn it. Use it. Repeat.

Short lessons for real-life money moments — from a surprise payout to a quiet week.

✳
🧭

Needs vs. wants

Make room for essentials and still enjoy the money you worked for.

A need is something important to cover, like travel to school, food, or a phone bill. A want is something nice to have. Before spending a payout, list the costs you must cover before your next income arrives. Then decide what is comfortable to spend.
🌦️

Plan for uneven income

A good week and a quiet week can both be part of gig work.

Try planning around your next essential expenses, not just the amount you received today. When a payout is bigger than usual, consider setting some aside for a quieter period. Start small and choose an amount you can realistically keep untouched.
🛟

Build a buffer

A small reserve can give you more choices when income slows down.

A buffer is money you keep for future essentials or unexpected costs. You can build it little by little. Keep it separate from everyday spending if that helps you avoid using it by accident. The right target depends on your expenses and situation.
🧾

Keep payout records

Simple records help you understand what you earn and what work costs.

Note the date, amount, and source each time money comes in. Keep receipts for work-related costs and check your records regularly. Tax rules depend on your circumstances and location, so ask a trusted adult or qualified tax professional when you need specific guidance.
