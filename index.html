1. Mastering Unpredictable Income & Safe Spend

Plan for busy months and quieter weeks.

Set up a drought buffer

  1. List essential monthly costs.
  2. Start with a realistic target, such as one week of essential costs, then build gradually.
  3. When a payout arrives, cover near-term essentials first and set aside a manageable amount.
  4. Review the plan after each payout and pause optional spending in quieter weeks.

Example: ₹6,000 in monthly essentials is about ₹1,500 per week. Your target depends on your circumstances.

2. Micro-Investing on Groww for Beginners

Understand SIPs and fund types before making decisions.

Before you invest

  1. Cover essential bills and build accessible emergency savings first.
  2. A SIP invests a set amount regularly. ₹100 may be possible for some options, but minimums depend on the fund and platform.
  3. Liquid funds hold short-term debt instruments but can still lose value; they are not bank deposits.
  4. Index funds track an index and can fall, especially over short periods.
  5. Only consider money you can leave invested for the appropriate time horizon. Never invest money needed for near-term essentials.

Educational only, not a recommendation. Returns are not guaranteed. This demo does not connect to Groww or place investments.

3. Tracking Market Patterns & Market Cycles

Learn the basics without assuming markets are predictable.

Read patterns carefully

  • A trend describes a direction over a chosen period; short-term moves don't prove a lasting pattern.
  • NAV means Net Asset Value, the per-unit value of a fund's assets after liabilities. A lower NAV does not automatically mean a fund is better value.
  • Markets move through expansions, declines, and recoveries, but timing cycles is difficult.
  • Diversification, time horizon, fees, and risk tolerance matter more than chasing a recent winner.
  • Past performance does not guarantee future returns. Avoid decisions based on one chart or social-media tip.
